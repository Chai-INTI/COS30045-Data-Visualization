Australian household energy

Know what your appliances are really costing you.

Electricity prices in the Australian market have climbed steadily over the past decade, yet most households can't say which appliances are driving their bill. This site walks through how everyday appliances — starting with televisions — convert watts into dollars, and gives you a calculator to work it out for your own home.
