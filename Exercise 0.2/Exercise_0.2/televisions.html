Televisions & Energy Consumption

Televisions are among the most visible energy users in household spaces. Compare the typical wattage of panel types below or check our visualisations in the menu.

43" LED — Everyday Living Room

Compact LED panel suited to a bedroom or second living area with modest power draw.

110W

65" LED — Family Room

Larger LED screen for a main living space; power draw rises with panel dimensions.

150W

55" OLED — Home Theatre

OLED panels draw more power at high peak brightness as each pixel emits light individually.

25W

32" LED — Kitchen / Study

Compact screen with the lowest power draw of the sample set.
