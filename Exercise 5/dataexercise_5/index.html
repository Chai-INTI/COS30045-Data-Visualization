TV Energy Consumption & Market Pricing

Explore energy consumption across TV display types, screen size market distribution in Australia, and historical Australian wholesale electricity spot prices.

55-inch TV Energy Consumption

Mean labelled energy consumption (kWh/year) for 55-inch models.

Among 55-inch TVs, LED consumes the most energy at 369 kWh/year, followed closely by OLED at 362 kWh/year, while LCD remains the most energy-efficient choice at 335 kWh/year.

Screen Size Market Share

Proportion of small, medium, and large television models in Australia.

Medium-sized TVs dominate the Australian market with 52.9% of all models, while large screens account for 30.0% and small screens make up the remaining 17.1%.

Australian Electricity Spot Prices (1998–2024)

Historical wholesale spot electricity prices in $ per MWh.

Australian wholesale electricity spot prices remained steady between 1998 and 2015, but have surged significantly in recent years, peaking at an average of $144.50 per MWh in 2022.

Data Sources: Australian TV Energy Rating Database and ARE Spot Price Records (1998–2024).
